# 27 · GOVERNANCE AND VERSIONING

## Durable changes requiring decision record

- Charter laws;
- kernel/run order;
- domain definitions;
- hard-gate effects;
- criterion scales/weights;
- evidence-path semantics;
- reliability thresholds;
- comparison/winner policy;
- output contract;
- privacy and connector policy.
