# ADR-2026-07-19 — Restore BNAT-50 inside Unified-1000

**Status:** PROPOSED_OWNER_REVIEW  
**Decision date:** 2026-07-19

## Context

The source Unified-1000 bank contained fifty scattered pseudo-BNAT entries. They preserved positional mixing but disclosed the test type, domain, compressed conflict, answer schema, extra twist, and variant marker. This changed the measured object from spontaneous actuation judgment to instruction following.

## Decision

1. Preserve all fifty original task positions and the overall 1,000-task count.
2. Put one exact canonical BNAT case 001-018 at the first occurrence of each family.
3. Replace the remaining thirty-two pseudo-BNAT entries with full neutral narrative mutations.
4. Keep all family labels, mechanisms, hashes, and lineage outside the candidate-facing bank.
5. Preserve the other 950 tasks unchanged.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Append 18 cases and create 1,018 tasks:** rejected because it changes bank size and downstream study identity.
- **Create a visible BNAT block:** rejected because it destroys positional mixing and evaluation blindness.
- **Sanitize the canonical 18:** rejected because the owner requested the full original canon.
- **Keep one-line pseudo variants:** rejected because they reveal the hidden construct and requested answer shape.

## Consequences

- The bank requires a new identity and new study ID.
- Historical results from the old Unified-1000 bank are not directly mergeable.
- Public canonical anchors remain contamination-prone.
- The evaluator-private directory becomes answer-key-adjacent material and must remain outside candidate sessions.
